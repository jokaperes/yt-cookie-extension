YouTube Cookie Exporter

Export your YouTube session cookies for use with yt-dlp on a server.

Cookies expiring fast? YouTube rotates cookies of sessions that stay open in the browser.
  1. Enable "Allow in Incognito" for this extension
  2. Open an incognito window and log in with a spare account
  3. Go to youtube.com/robots.txt
  4. Click Export here
  5. Close the incognito window and never log in with that account in a browser again
Warning: Cookies are like session tokens. Don't share them publicly. Delete after use.
